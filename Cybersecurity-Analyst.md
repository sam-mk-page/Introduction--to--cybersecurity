# Cybersecurity Analyst

## 1. What is the name of the career path?
Cybersecurity Analyst

## 2. Main responsibilities
- Monitor and protect company networks, systems, and data from cyber threats
- Investigate security alerts and suspicious activity
- Perform vulnerability assessments and risk analysis
- Develop security policies and educate staff on cybersecurity best practices
- Respond to incidents and help recover affected systems
- Stay updated on the latest hacking techniques and security trends

## 3. Skills required
- Networking and understanding of Windows/Linux operating systems
- Knowledge of firewalls, VPNs, and intrusion detection systems
- Problem-solving and analytical thinking
- Understanding of common threats: phishing, malware, ransomware, DDoS
- Good communication and report writing skills
- Certifications like CompTIA Security+ and Network+

## 4. Tools or technologies commonly used
- SIEM Tools: Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, IBM QRadar
- Network Tools: Wireshark, Nmap
- Vulnerability Scanners**: Nessus, OpenVAS
- Security Software: Firewalls, Antivirus, EDR
- Operating Systems: Windows, Linux, Kali Linux

## 5. Why did I choose this career path?
I chose Cybersecurity Analyst because I love protecting people's devices and businesses from cyber attacks. I enjoy problem-solving and learning how hackers think. This role gives me a strong foundation in cybersecurity and allows me to explore other areas like ethical hacking later.

## 6. Steps to become successful in this field
- Learn IT and networking fundamentals
- Earn CompTIA Security+ certification
- Practice hands-on skills on TryHackMe and CyberDefenders
- Build projects and share them on GitHub
- Join cybersecurity communities in Kenya
- Apply for internships and entry-level analyst jobs
